CHAPTER 3  ·  1 OF 4

How Israeli funds position themselves

Our working read of the archetypes operating in this market. A lens, not a verdict — correct it as we go.

Archetype

Where it wins

Where it is exposed

Specialist

Founders in that category call first

Exposed when the category cools

Company builder

Conviction and ownership from day zero

Reads as competing with founders

Global bridge

Commercial traction, not introductions

Only matters if that market is on the roadmap

Founder’s fund

Mindshare and inbound at seed

Thin beyond the individual

Platform

Later rounds and portfolio support

Slower, less personal

Contrarian

Distinct thesis, better entry price

Harder LP story without realised proof

Institution

Credibility with boards and LPs

Reads conservative to ambitious founders
